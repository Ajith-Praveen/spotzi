Problem Statement

Detecting suspicious claims is only the beginning. Connecting the evidence is the real challenge.

Key challenges in healthcare claims investigation.

01

Alert Overload

High volumes of flagged claims make it difficult to identify and prioritize cases that require immediate attention.

02

Disconnected Signals

Unusual billing patterns, shifts in provider behaviour and hidden relationships often remain fragmented, making broader suspicious patterns harder to uncover.

03

Lack of Explainable Evidence

Risk scores alone cannot explain why a claim was flagged, what evidence supports the concern or what steps investigators should take next.

04

Risk of False Positives

Legitimate claims may appear suspicious. Without examining context and alternative explanations, investigators risk treating unusual activity as evidence of fraud.
