# SpotZⁱ — Accuracy evaluation on independent public data

## Why this exists
SpotZⁱ's own demo data was generated by us, with fraud scenarios written alongside the rules, so its near-perfect scores prove little. To measure accuracy on data we did **not** create, we use two public synthetic datasets as the *background* (normal) claims, inject known fraud schemes plus legitimate look-alikes, and score the full, unchanged SpotZⁱ pipeline.

## Method
1. **Import** public synthetic claims unchanged: CMS DE-SynPUF Sample 1 (2008–2010 Medicare claims; hospitals and outpatient facilities) and the Synthea sample (clinicians, patients, organisations).
2. **Inject** labelled schemes into a few providers, each starting at a random month and continuing to the end of the data:
   - `dup` duplicate submissions · `units` unit inflation · `repeat` panels re-billed within days · `upcode` visits shifted to level 5 · `deceased` services after death
   - **`influx` — held out: no rule targets it** (a wave of new out-of-area patients, each receiving one identical bundle)
   - **benign look-alikes**: legitimate growth (new local patients with complete, realistic care episodes) — these should *not* be escalated
3. **Run** the full pipeline. Labels are used only for scoring and to train the forecast; never for detection.
4. **Score**: did each injected provider become a case? how well does each detector rank bad providers (AUC)? precision of the SIU queue? were look-alikes escalated? forecast AUC on a later, purged time window.

## Results

Thresholds were chosen on injection seed 21; **the numbers below are from a fresh injection (seed 99) with different providers and onsets**, so they were not used to tune anything.

## What this shows
- **The fused Nexus Brain ranks injected bad providers far better than rules alone** (DE-SynPUF AUC 1.00 vs 0.94 for rules; Synthea 0.95 vs 0.83).
- **Upcoding and unit inflation are now caught at low-volume institutions** (DE-SynPUF 3/3 each).
- **False escalations are low on the realistic dataset**: 0 of 4 legitimate-growth facilities escalated; 84% of all opened cases are true.
- **The top of the queue is reliable** (precision @5 = 100% on both datasets), which matters most with limited investigator capacity.

## Known weaknesses (measured, not hidden)
- **Precision/recall trade-off on the thin dataset**: the stricter lead threshold (fused score ≥ 0.97) removed false leads but also the held-out *influx* scheme on Synthea (0/2; median rank 14 of 200). On DE-SynPUF influx is still caught 2/3.
- **Claim-line recall is low** (~0.2): SpotZⁱ finds *providers worth reviewing*, it does not label every line.
- **Synthea is thin** (≈ one clinician per patient): benign look-alikes cannot be tested there.

## Changes made because of this evaluation (general rules, not tuned to specific providers)
1. Duplicate rule treats the **later-paid** submission as the duplicate.
2. **Data-driven unit limits** (99th percentile per code) in addition to the fixed limit table.
3. **Quarterly upcoding view**: ≥ 8 visits in 90 days with level-5 share ≥ max(50%, 3× peers) — catches low-volume upcoders the monthly rule cannot see.
4. **Concentrated-findings case trigger**: a provider with ≥ 5 flagged lines making up ≥ 40% of its recent billing is opened even at low volume.
5. **Brain leads need near-unanimous consensus** (fused ≥ 0.97 instead of 0.90): at 0.90 a 4,332-provider portfolio produced ~30 false leads.
6. Change-point detector weighs **composition change** (service mix, out-of-area patients) above raw growth.
7. Importers merge repeated codes on one claim into **units** (CMS and Synthea both encode units that way).

Evaluation-setup corrections (not product changes): schemes sized to be material (≥ 30 lines), legitimate growth built from **complete real patient episodes**, and synthetic deaths assigned only to patients with no later genuine care.

## Limits of this evidence
Injected schemes are our own models of fraud. Results show SpotZⁱ detects *these* patterns against independent background data; they are **not** real-world fraud detection rates. Real labelled outcomes (e.g. the Kaggle provider-fraud set with your token, or a payer's closed SIU cases) are the next step.

*Reproduce:* `cd spotzi && python3 evaluate_public.py synthea synpuf` (results in `spotzi/data/evaluation/`).
